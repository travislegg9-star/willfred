How to play

  • Pull back anywhere on screen like a slingshot, then release — that's Woofa's throw.
  • Land it in the glowing sweet-spot ring and a kid leaps for the hero catch.
  • From Level 3 the catcher runs back and forth — lead your throw to where they'll be!
  • Watch the wind gauge up top — green carries the throw, orange fights it. Aim off to beat it.
  • Blow the throw and the kid faceplants — breaks their bones and cries. Oops.
  • Perfect catches earn the most 🦴 bones. Build a 🔥 streak for bonuses.
  • Spend bones in the 🛒 shop — better balls, and a Batman cape for the kids.
  • Overshoot on the rooftops and it's gone — you're stuck with the sad tennis ball till you buy a new one.
